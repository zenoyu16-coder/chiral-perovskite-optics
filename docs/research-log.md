# Research log

## 2026-10-20 — Baseline

Aim: Establish a reliable structural baseline for chiral layered halide semiconductors before designing any new molecules, DFT, MD or ML.

Selected model: S-MBA2PbI4 experimental temperature series (298/200/100 K) from Jana et al. (2020). The R/S paired comparison is **not yet available in the local manifest**.

First computation: per-Pb unit-cell volume, Pb–I bond statistics, PbI6 cis-angle variance, and symmetry classification. Source CIF identifiers and SHA-256 retained.

Falsifiability: Differences among the S temperature points, if present, are thermal/structural changes; they must **not** be described as induced by R/S chirality. A Sohncke classification is not proof of measured intrinsic CD.

Next decision: acquire a genuinely matched R/S crystal and optical pair. If unavailable, explore the available temperature series and published spectra as a learning exercise, without inventing a universal design rule.

Stop rule: If comparisons depend on incompatible phases or missing orientation controls, document the gap and change to a better-characterized material rather than increasing model complexity.
