# Optical comparison protocol (planned, not measured)

The structural pilot contains **no CD or PL data**. When adding real measurements, log at minimum:

- Sample ID; composition; nominal R/S/rac identity; synthetic batch and enantiomeric purity when known.
- Temperature, phase and XRD orientation; film thickness; substrate; solvent and processing method.
- Spectral observable and units (OD, CD mdeg, LD, PL intensity, quantum yield).
- Wavelength calibration, path geometry, sample rotation/azimuth, front/back acquisition.
- Raw instrument exports, background/substrate files, and source DOI.
- Date, processing code/version and uncertainty or repeatability estimate.

Minimum quality controls for a chiroptical hypothesis:

1. Compare **R and S of matched phase and processing**, plus a suitable achiral/racemic control when it forms a comparable phase.
2. Measure sample rotation dependence and front/back spectra, and inspect **LD and linear birefringence** contamination. CD sign reversal alone is suggestive but insufficient in oriented films.
3. Report absorption and film thickness along with CD; distinguish instrumental ellipticity from intrinsic complex refractive-index response.
4. Never assign a microscopic Rashba/CISS/exciton mechanism from a CD line shape alone.

- Scalon et al., *Advanced Optical Materials*, doi:10.1002/adom.202300776
- *Nature Communications* (2024), doi:10.1038/s41467-024-46851-2
- Sheikh et al. *Chemical Communications* (2022), doi:10.1039/D2CC01811J
